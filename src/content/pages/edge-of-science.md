Have you ever felt a deep connection to a certain place—or an inexplicable resistance to another? Location astrology, also known as astrocartography, offers powerful insights into how different geographic locations resonate with your unique birth chart. By mapping planetary lines across the globe, astrocartography reveals where love might blossom, career opportunities may thrive, or spiritual growth is most supported.

Location astrology analysis is part of the [Extended natal chart analysis](/astrological-consultation/).
